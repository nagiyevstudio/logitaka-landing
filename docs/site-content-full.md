## FAQ

1. **What is Logitaka Talk?** — A fullscreen voice workspace. Speak naturally and Logitaka assembles your words into structured draft cards — tasks, events, decisions, notes — in real time. Review and confirm to apply.

2. **Does it write to my projects while I speak?** — No. Everything you see while speaking is a draft. Nothing touches your workspace until you hit Apply — then Logitaka executes exactly the batch you reviewed, with no second AI pass.

3. **What if it misunderstands me?** — Corrections happen in place. Say "not tomorrow, Friday" and the card updates. Missing fields show as empty slots you can fill with your next phrase.

4. **How long is a session?** — Up to 3 minutes or 8 actions per batch, whichever comes first. Apply, then start a new one.

5. **Which AI models power it?** — Logitaka routes requests across 43 active models from 8 providers — including OpenAI, Google, Anthropic, Mistral, Alibaba, xAI, Moonshot, and Xiaomi. The system automatically selects the right model based on task complexity, balancing speed and cost.

6. **Is Logitaka a chatbot?** — No. Logitaka Talk is a deterministic voice workspace, not a chat interface. When you confirm a batch, Logitaka executes exactly what you reviewed — there is no second AI pass and no reinterpretation at apply time.

7. **What plans include Talk?** — Talk is available on every tier — Starter, Pro, and Premium. Inbox, Focus, Discussion, and Structured Meetings are Pro and above. DevTeam/GitHub, AI Strategist Insights, Personal AI Profiler, and Independent AI Analytics are Premium only.

8. **How is my data handled?** — Payments are processed securely via Lemon Squeezy — Logitaka never sees or stores your card details. Have strict compliance needs or want a custom deployment? Contact us at support@logitaka.com.

9. **Who is Logitaka for?** — Founders, managers, and freelancers who think faster than they type. If you constantly have more to track than you have time to write down, Talk was built for you.
